# Privacy

Spectrum: Monitor Color Profiles Manager collects no personal data and makes no network connections.

It reads your monitors' EDID and the color settings Windows reports, writes color profiles to the Windows color folder (`%SystemRoot%\System32\spool\drivers\color`), and keeps its settings, a log and profile backups in its own app data folder on your PC. None of this leaves your computer.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/AndreaTorti-01/Spectrum/issues).
