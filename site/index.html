Your monitor already knows its colors.

Spectrum decodes the EDID your monitor reports, writes an ICC profile from it and keeps your SDR and HDR profiles applied.

EDID · bytes 0x19–0x22

Ten bytes define the gamut.

Spectrum decodes them and the gamma at byte 0x17, then writes an ICC v4 profile. ArgyllCMS and DisplayCAL aren't needed.
